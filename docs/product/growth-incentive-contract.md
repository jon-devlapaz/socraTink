# Growth-Incentive Compatibility Contract

Status: Accepted baseline for Wayfinder issue #18.

This contract defines how Socratink revenue, growth, notification, retention, analytics, access, and success decisions remain compatible with learner independence. It prevents commercial success from depending on unnecessary assistance, hidden persuasion, emotional attachment, prevented exit, or the misuse of learner-owned state.

It is governed by [`north-star.md`](north-star.md). Learner-owned state and continuity remain governed by [`learner-state-contract.md`](learner-state-contract.md). Motivation and relationship boundaries remain governed by [`motivation-contract.md`](motivation-contract.md). Instruction, assistance, evaluation, and evidence remain governed by [`teaching-skill-contract.md`](teaching-skill-contract.md). Learning Targets and route decisions remain governed by [`learning-map-contract.md`](learning-map-contract.md).

This is a product and governance contract. It does not establish that a business model is viable, that an intervention improves learning, or that a usage pattern reveals learner motivation or dependency. Those remain empirical questions.

## Central decision

Socratink may earn revenue from capability-building, trustworthy continuity, and transparent access. It must not earn by maximizing attention, message volume, session length, emotional attachment, unnecessary assistance, prevented exit, or resistance to support fading.

A learner succeeding outside the product, seeking human help, using less instructional assistance, pausing, changing direction, exporting state, or leaving after accomplishing a purpose can be a successful outcome. None is automatically a failure of retention.

The permanent business model is deferred until the smallest complete product proof demonstrates learner value without violating agency, truthfulness, accessibility, privacy, or safety guardrails.

## Non-negotiable compatibility rules

1. Commercial objectives cannot override learner goals, pedagogy, evidence validity, accessibility, safety, privacy, or learner ownership.
2. Product return, continued use, purchase, attachment, and willingness to recommend are not learner-capability evidence.
3. Reduced usage, support fading, human referral, off-product action, pause, export, and departure are not inherently negative outcomes.
4. Continued use is not inherently positive. It requires a learner-declared need or other attributable purpose and demonstrated value.
5. No usage pattern alone establishes motivation, satisfaction, dissatisfaction, dependency, independence, or vulnerability.
6. The product may provide long-term continuity without manufacturing dependence. Continuity is not a scaffold that must disappear.
7. Commercial variables may control transparent service entitlement. Within the same declared entitlement and learner context, they may not change pedagogy, assistance, evidence interpretation, route selection, motivation behavior, or exit friction.
8. Learner-owned state cannot become an acquisition, persuasion, pricing, or sales-segmentation asset.
9. Commercial viability cannot compensate for failed learner value or a material guardrail breach.
10. Learner value cannot excuse a manipulative, inaccessible, deceptive, or dependency-producing mechanism.

## Non-compensating metric architecture

Socratink rejects one composite north-star metric for product governance. The following channels remain separate:

| Channel | What it may establish | What it must not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Learner value** | A declared product hypothesis improved its direct outcome, such as delayed performance, transfer, learner-created work, useful action, or performance under reduced or declared assistance | General capability, satisfaction, retention value, or business viability from one proxy |
| **Agency and mission** | The learner could choose, correct, override, pause, seek human help, act outside the product, complete a purpose, or leave without penalty | That more departure or less support is always better |
| **Business viability** | Revenue, cost to serve, conversion, refund, and operating constraints support a sustainable offer | That the learning mechanism works or is safe |
| **Guardrails** | Trust, truthfulness, accessibility, privacy, safety, correction cost, manipulation concerns, and subgroup harms remain within declared bounds | A tradeable score that stronger revenue or learning results can offset |
| **Usage diagnostics** | Descriptive facts about sessions, returns, feature use, delivery, and system operation | Motivation, dependency, capability, satisfaction, or success without additional evidence |

Every material learner-facing or product-mechanism hypothesis must name its direct learner-value outcome, relevant guardrails, observation period, comparison or baseline, uncertainty, disconfirming result, and decision rule before results are interpreted. A business-only operational hypothesis must instead name its direct business outcome and prove that it cannot alter learner-facing behavior, learner-owned state, access rights, or contract-governed decisions.

A learner-facing or product-mechanism decision may proceed only when:

1. the declared learner-value threshold is met;
2. the offer is economically viable enough for its current stage;
3. no material agency, trust, safety, accessibility, privacy, or truthfulness guardrail is breached; and
4. negative results, non-returners, corrections, exclusions, and subgroup differences remain visible.

## Outcome semantics

Socratink records usage and lifecycle outcomes as evidence-sourced, non-ordinal states rather than a retention ladder.

Possible outcomes include:

- continuing because an active learner-chosen need remains;
- returning with a new or renewed purpose;
- completing a goal and acting outside Socratink;
- pausing intentionally;
- changing the goal, modality, Teaching Skill, or support source;
- seeking a teacher, peer, mentor, clinician, institution, or community;
- leaving because the purpose was accomplished;
- leaving because value was not demonstrated;
- leaving for cost, access, trust, privacy, accessibility, or unknown reasons.

Returning is not automatically better than leaving after success. Leaving after success is not automatically better than continuing to pursue a real need.

Reasons must come from a learner declaration or an attributable factual event. Telemetry must not diagnose why a learner continued or left. A non-returner may be contacted only under an applicable consented communication purpose and cadence. Silence must remain unknown rather than being converted into a motivation or dependency claim.

## Assistance, support, and dependency

The product must distinguish four categories:

1. **Instructional assistance** helps with target-relevant learning work. It may decrease, increase, or change form according to the Learning Target, task, prior evidence, and declared conditions.
2. **Accessibility accommodation** provides equivalent access. It is not evidence of reduced capability, a premium feature to surcharge, or a scaffold to remove.
3. **Execution support** helps accomplish an external goal under an explicit division of cognitive labor. It may remain useful even when independent learning performance grows.
4. **Relationship and state continuity** preserves learner-owned context, preferences, evidence, and chosen Persona relationship. It is not instructional dependence.

Independence is evaluated per Learning Target and performance condition, not from global product use. Frequent return, long-duration study, spaced practice, accommodation, tool use, continuing Persona relationships, or requests for explanation do not establish dependency by themselves.

A dependency concern requires attributable evidence of a harmful relationship or product effect, such as:

- reduced willingness to act without unnecessary product mediation;
- interference with human support or relationships;
- coercive, guilt-producing, exclusive, or escalating interaction;
- assistance that conceals or replaces target-relevant learner work;
- exit friction or state hostage-taking;
- a product decision designed to preserve usage against the learner's interest.

A suspected dependency concern must remain transparent, correctable, non-diagnostic, and governed by the motivation, learner-state, and Teaching Skill contracts.

## Notification and re-engagement authority

Every notification must declare one typed purpose:

- **learner-authored reminder**;
- **consented learning opportunity**, such as a due reconstruction under an active learner plan;
- **requested continuity update**;
- **transactional service notice**, such as security, billing, privacy, or material service status.

A learning item becoming due does not by itself authorize contact. The learner must also have consented to the channel and cadence.

Notifications must have:

- an attributable purpose;
- explicit channel and cadence authority where applicable;
- an expiry condition;
- privacy-safe presentation;
- a visible disable or change path;
- no persuasive escalation after dismissal or non-response.

Notifications must not use hidden emotional inference, vulnerability, Persona attachment, guilt, streak loss, artificial urgency, or repeated interruption to produce return. Transactional notices cannot be disguised re-engagement.

## Learner-state and commercial data boundary

Canonical learner state includes Learner Evidence, goals, Sources, Learning Maps, Purpose Threads, Motivation Hypotheses, Persona Relationship State, Attempts, Evidence Records, and other learner-owned continuity. Commercial acquisition and sales systems are denied access by default.

A governed analytics path may receive a purpose-limited projection only when it has:

- named fields and derivation rules;
- a declared evaluation or operational purpose;
- data minimization;
- retention and deletion rules;
- access controls;
- versioned metric definitions;
- no commercial write-back into learner state, pedagogy, evidence, or motivation behavior.

Separately consented commercial facts, such as billing identity, purchased entitlement, or an explicitly submitted sales inquiry, remain commercial records rather than learner evidence.

An explicitly stated learning goal does not become commercial data merely because the learner disclosed it to Socratink. Reuse for a commercial purpose requires a separate, understandable action and must not condition core learning quality on disclosure.

## Causal noninterference

Holding entitled capability, learner context, active goal, safety conditions, and product version constant, commercial variables must not alter:

- Teaching Skill eligibility or ranking;
- task difficulty or challenge selection;
- assistance, reveal, or solution policy;
- Evidence Contract interpretation;
- accepted learner claims;
- Motivation Recovery behavior;
- human-help recommendations;
- export, correction, deletion, or exit friction;
- the truthfulness of explanations or progress representation.

Payment and entitlement may transparently determine whether a declared service is available, including hosted inference, storage, or a premium capability. The unavailable capability and its consequence must be visible before purchase and at the decision point. Entitlement must not be translated into worse pedagogy, weaker evidence standards, manipulation, or state hostage-taking.

Each material commercial or growth release must include a noninterference check that compares decision outputs under changed commercial variables while holding authorized educational inputs constant. Any unexplained change blocks release.

## Experiments and release gates

These constraints must be enforced through procedure and technical boundaries rather than founder intention alone.

Before observing experiment results, preserve a versioned manifest containing:

- hypothesis and mechanism;
- eligible population and exclusions;
- intervention and comparison;
- direct learner-value outcome;
- agency, trust, safety, accessibility, privacy, and truthfulness guardrails;
- metric definitions and data sources;
- observation window;
- thresholds, stop gates, and decision rule;
- evaluator and analysis version;
- known limitations.

The evidence burden increases when a change can:

- alter pedagogy, assistance, evidence, access, price, notification authority, consent, or learner ownership;
- use sensitive or longitudinal state;
- create persuasive or relationship effects;
- affect a high-consequence learner decision;
- produce a broad product or marketing claim.

This contract does not create a second consequence-tier taxonomy. Existing contract-specific tiers and validity rules remain authoritative.

Formative interface and comprehension changes may use bounded qualitative evidence. Changes to learning, access, pricing, notification, state use, or safety require representative workflow tests and explicit guardrails. Broad causal claims require an appropriate comparison and independent analysis. A solo founder is not required to run every iteration like a clinical trial, but consequence cannot be hidden by calling a material intervention formative.

## First paid-offer hypothesis

The permanent business model remains undecided. After the actual-value proof clears its learner-value and guardrail thresholds, Socratink may test one bounded, fixed-price **Learning Project**.

The first offer must have:

- one coherent learner-declared goal and understandable scope;
- a fixed, transparent price with no automatic renewal;
- no per-message, time-spent, intervention, Attempt, Evidence Record, or assistance charge;
- adaptation within the agreed goal without manufacturing another purchase;
- a transparent re-scope, learner agreement, and new price before material goal expansion;
- pauses and irregular schedules that do not forfeit scope, evidence, or continuity through artificial deadlines;
- no surcharge for accessibility accommodations or equivalent-access modalities;
- persistent learner-owned continuity across projects and purchase states;
- usable export, correction, and deletion rights independent of repurchase;
- a clear completion condition that does not require continued product use.

The test must observe affordability and exclusion without reducing educational quality for slower learners, learners needing accommodation, or learners with limited income. The MVP does not promise a universal subsidy or institutional access model, but it must preserve access failures as product evidence rather than excluding them from evaluation.

A new paid project must follow a new or materially expanded learner purpose. Socratink must not manufacture projects, withhold completion, fragment a coherent goal, or create artificial expiration to generate another charge.

## Truthful growth and value communication

Socratink may grow by making demonstrated value understandable to well-matched learners.

Allowed practices include:

- explaining the product mechanism and limitations plainly;
- publishing scoped, reproducible results with population, task, assistance, timing, uncertainty, and negative findings;
- using learner-authorized testimonials that do not expose canonical learner state or imply unsupported outcomes;
- inviting a learner to share a result or referral without reward pressure, hidden access consequences, or Persona influence;
- describing who the product currently supports and who it does not yet support.

Prohibited practices include:

- manufactured virality, streak pressure, artificial scarcity, guilt, attachment, or fear of losing the Agent;
- targeting based on Learner Evidence, Motivation Hypotheses, Purpose Threads, relationship state, inferred vulnerability, or hidden affect;
- presenting engagement, fluency, completion, or testimonials as proof of capability;
- suppressing failures, non-returners, subgroup harms, correction cost, or accessibility exclusions;
- claiming universal learning effectiveness from a narrow proof.

## Continuity, access, and exit

Learner-owned continuity must survive pause, purchase changes, model changes, project completion, and business-model experiments subject only to disclosed service and storage constraints.

The learner must retain practical paths to:

- inspect and correct state;
- export supported state and artifacts;
- delete learner-owned state;
- decline marketing and non-transactional contact;
- stop using a Persona or motivation behavior;
- seek human support;
- complete a purpose and leave without penalty.

Socratink may charge for an ongoing service. It may not charge the learner to recover ownership rights, correct false state, end manipulation, or escape avoidable lock-in.

## Acceptance boundary

A growth, revenue, pricing, notification, retention, or analytics mechanism is compatible only if all of the following are true:

1. It earns from capability-building, trustworthy continuity, or transparent access rather than attention or prevented exit.
2. Learner value, agency, business viability, guardrails, and usage diagnostics remain non-compensating channels.
3. Continued use, return, pause, human handoff, off-product action, and departure retain evidence-sourced, non-ordinal meanings.
4. Instructional assistance may adapt per target, while accessibility, execution support, and chosen continuity are not treated as scaffolds to eliminate.
5. No usage pattern alone establishes dependency.
6. Notifications have typed purposes, appropriate consent, expiry, privacy-safe presentation, and no persuasive escalation.
7. Commercial systems cannot consume canonical learner state. Governed analytics receives only a purpose-limited projection with no commercial write-back.
8. Within the same declared entitlement, commercial variables cannot alter pedagogy, assistance, evidence, motivation behavior, human referral, or exit.
9. Entitlement differences are transparent and do not weaken learner ownership or evidence validity.
10. Experiment outcomes, guardrails, exclusions, thresholds, and stop gates are frozen before results are interpreted.
11. Evidence requirements scale with consequence without introducing a competing consequence taxonomy.
12. The first paid offer, if tested, is a bounded fixed-price Learning Project only after actual learner value is demonstrated.
13. Project scope, pause, accessibility, continuity, and completion rules do not punish slower learning or manufacture additional purchases.
14. Growth communication uses scoped demonstrated value without learner-state exploitation, manufactured virality, or unsupported claims.
15. The learner can become more independent, seek human help, act outside Socratink, complete a purpose, export state, or leave without making the product or business treat that success as failure.

Failure of any item blocks the mechanism or requires an explicit contract amendment. Revenue or engagement cannot compensate for failure.

## Explicit deferrals

This contract does not yet choose:

- the permanent subscription, project, institutional, local-model, or hybrid business model;
- final prices, margins, subsidies, or cost ceilings;
- a universal access or institutional deployment program;
- production analytics infrastructure or vendors;
- marketing channels or growth targets;
- population-wide causal claims;
- minors, protected-character commerce, Persona marketplaces, or Voice Package monetization.

Those decisions must preserve this contract or amend it explicitly with the affected trade-off, evidence, migration, and learner consequence.
